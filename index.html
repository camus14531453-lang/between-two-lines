About the farm

Kentish Town City Farm opened in 1972 — the first city farm in the UK. It began when InterAction, a community theatre group, rented a disused timber yard from Camden Council, wedged where two railway lines cross. Today it is a local charity, free to enter, for people in the city to meet animals, grow food and learn.

A free farm runs on giving. A donation station stands by the main building; a red post box asks for post donations.

Which leaves a question I couldn’t answer on the walk: is this place meeting a need — or is it designed to make us want a certain kind of city life?
